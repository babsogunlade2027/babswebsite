# BABS Website — Editorial Guidelines

## Accuracy
Verify names, titles, dates, locations, figures, quotations and claims. Attribute statements and statistics to their originating office, document or speaker. Do not fabricate endorsements, attendance, achievements or project delivery.

## Campaign language
FOCUS means **Forward-thinking Opportunities for Community Unity and Sustainability**.

Tagline: **Building the Future. Empowering the People. Advancing Ibarapa East**.

Manifesto: **The Accelerated Pathway to Progress: The BABS Strategic Blueprint for Ibarapa East**.

Describe manifesto commitments as proposals, priorities, intentions or policy positions. Never present proposed programmes as completed projects.

## Publication workflow
1. Draft.
2. Fact-check and source.
3. Editorial review.
4. Approval by the authorised campaign office.
5. Publish and retain the source record.

Use British English and accessible language. Correct material errors promptly.
