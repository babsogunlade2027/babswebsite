About Babatunde

Biography

A business and finance professional committed to public service.

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade, widely known as BABS, is a business and finance professional whose public platform centres on community engagement, responsible representation and practical development conversations for Ibarapa East.

The constituency covers Eruwa and Lanlate communities across ten wards: Eruwa wards 1–6 and Lanlate wards 7–10.

A full, verified biography, education record and professional history will be published here once approved by the campaign office.
