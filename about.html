About Babatunde

A Builder’s Discipline. A Son of Eruwa’s Heart. A Representative for Every Ward of Ibarapa East.

Meet BABS

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade

Ibarapa East is a constituency of farmers who feed the state, traders who keep our markets alive, artisans who build and mend, teachers who shape the next generation and young people who are impatient for opportunity. It deserves a representative who understands this life from the inside, and who has the competence to carry it into the Oyo State House of Assembly.

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade, known to supporters as BABS, offers that combination: deep roots in Eruwa and Lanlate, a professional career in accounting and business management, more than two decades of service within the All Progressives Congress, and a stated commitment to serve every community in the constituency. He is the APC candidate for the Oyo State House of Assembly, Ibarapa East State Constituency, in the 2027 elections.

Vision to Lead. Capacity to Deliver.

Rooted in Eruwa and Ibarapa East

A son of Eruwa, Hon. Ogunlade grew up with ties to the community networks, markets, schools, farms and traditional institutions that give Ibarapa its character. That upbringing taught him what residents already know: that a motorable road, a working clinic, a well-equipped classroom and a safe water source are not abstractions. They are the difference between a hard day and a better one.

He sees Ibarapa East by its strengths as much as by its challenges: hardworking farmers and traders, enterprising artisans and professionals, respected traditional institutions, strong community associations and a young population ready to contribute. His belief is simple. A constituency with these assets needs a representative who will speak up for it, work with it and answer to it.

A Lifelong Commitment to Learning

Hon. Ogunlade’s academic record reflects a progression of study across accounting and finance:

  • Apode High School
  • National Diploma in Accounting, Federal Polytechnic, Ede (2002)
  • Higher National Diploma in Accounting, The Polytechnic, Ibadan (2006)
  • B.Sc. Accounting, Crescent University (2020)
  • M.Sc. Accounting, University of Lagos (2025)

This grounding in accounting informs his stated approach to public responsibility: respect for the proper use of resources, attention to detail and the belief that every naira entrusted to government should be accounted for.

Management Experience That Turns Plans into Results

Beyond the classroom, Hon. Ogunlade has led and managed in the private sector. He is Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of Skylark Homes & Properties Ltd, and previously served as Deputy Managing Director and General Manager, Finance, at Portal Realities (2020 to 2024).

These roles have given him experience in financial administration, business development, organisational planning and the daily link between resources and results. In the House of Assembly, that experience can inform engagement on budget priorities, public spending, enterprise development and oversight, within the constitutional duties of the legislature.

A Political Journey Built on Loyalty, Reconciliation and Service

Hon. Ogunlade identifies with the All Progressives Congress (APC) and states that his membership dates to 2003. Over the years, he says he has remained engaged with ward structures, party members and community stakeholders, understanding that relationships and trust are built over time, not over a campaign season.

He emerged as the party’s candidate for Ibarapa East State Constituency following the APC direct primary election. He has pledged to work with party members across past differences so that the party approaches 2027 united.

On the Ground, Among the People

His campaign describes recent engagements with faith leaders and traditional councils, the National Association of Lanlate Students, APC youths at the Ibarapa East Youth Summit, community groups including the Water Base Group and the Anunimorigba Landlord Association in Lanlate, and participants at the unveiling of the 2027 CAN Calendar in Aborerin Zone, Eruwa.

The campaign has also announced a ward tour and empowerment programme covering all ten wards, with support intended for widows, older residents and people facing hardship. In November 2026, campaign communications announced approval and drilling of three boreholes across Eruwa and Lanlate, reporting work at the third location in Anunimorigba Community, Lanlate, as complete. These are campaign-reported updates; project status should be read in that context.

A Leadership Philosophy: Listen. Think. Advocate. Answer.

  • Listen — to farmers, traders, students, elders, women and young people in every ward, not only at election time.
  • Think — before promising. Development in education, agriculture, infrastructure, healthcare and enterprise is connected, and sound choices require sound planning.
  • Advocate — through lawmaking, budget consideration, oversight and engagement with the agencies responsible for delivery.
  • Answer — to the people through regular town hall meetings and public reports showing what has been proposed, approved, started and completed.

He recognises that a legislator does not run every ministry or independently build every road. A determined, informed and accessible representative can, however, make the constituency’s case where decisions are made, scrutinise public resources and keep the people informed.

A Mandate for the Whole Constituency

The APC is the platform through which Hon. Ogunlade seeks the people’s mandate, but representation does not stop at party lines. If elected, he says he will serve every constituent in Eruwa, Lanlate and neighbouring communities, including those who voted for someone else. His stated commitment is to balanced development, transparent priorities and an accessible office.
