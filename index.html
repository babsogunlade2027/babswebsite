Meet BABS

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade (affectionately known as BABS) is a committed public servant, visionary leader, and grassroots politician dedicated to the socio-economic transformation of Ibarapa East State Constituency.

Deeply rooted in the community, Hon. Ogunlade understands the everyday challenges and immense potentials of our people—from hardworking farmers in our agrarian belt to vibrant youths eager for opportunities, and market women driving local enterprise. His political philosophy is built on the bedrock of integrity, accountability, and inclusive participation.

As the APC candidate, he represents a fresh paradigm of legislative advocacy: one that bridges the gap between the grassroots and state governance to deliver sustainable development.

The Vision

The FOCUS Legislative Blueprint

Hon. Ogunlade’s legislative agenda is anchored on the FOCUS framework, designed to address the foundational needs of Ibarapa East.

F

Farming & Food Security

The Goal

Empowering local farmers with modern agricultural inputs, better market access, storage infrastructure, and cooperative support to make Ibarapa East the food basket of Oyo State.

O

Opportunity & Jobs

The Goal

Creating sustainable economic opportunities through youth skill acquisition programs, digital training hubs, and startup grants for local entrepreneurs and artisans.

C

Community Infrastructure

The Goal

Lobbying and partnering with relevant agencies to improve rural-urban roads, expand potable water supply, and boost community electrification.

U

Uplifting Education & Health

The Goal

Upgrading primary healthcare centers, providing medical outreach for vulnerable populations, and supporting local schools with educational materials and infrastructure grants.

S

Security & Social Cohesion

The Goal

Fostering peace and security through active collaboration with traditional rulers, security agencies, and community policing structures.

News, Press Releases & Updates

The 2026 Archive

Statements, engagements and milestones from the campaign trail across Ibarapa East, newest first.

  1. Late October 2026
    Campaign Council Statement

    Ibarapa East APC Campaign Council Issues Advisory Statement

    Following local political tensions, the Ibarapa East APC Campaign Council issued an advisory statement warning opposing elements against provoking political chaos. The council explicitly rallied public backing behind legitimate APC candidates, including Asiwaju Bola Ahmed Tinubu, Sen. Sharafadeen Alli, Hon. Remi Oseni, Hon. Asimiyu Yusuf Osuolale, and Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade.

  2. October 1, 2026
    National Message

    Nigeria’s 66th Independence Anniversary Message

    Released through BABS Media, Hon. Ogunlade felicitated with citizens on Nigeria’s 66th Independence Anniversary. He urged constituents to maintain unity, peaceful coexistence, and faith in good governance, concluding with shared prayers for the progress of Ibarapa East, Oyo State, and the nation.

  3. September 20 – 27, 2026
    Community Outreach

    Ibarapa East Ward Tour & Empowerment Scheme

    Hon. Ogunlade’s campaign rolled out a comprehensive, week-long empowerment programme and ten-ward tour spanning all neighborhoods of Ibarapa East. Designed to directly engage constituents, the initiative combined open interactive town halls with welfare packages for widows, the elderly, and vulnerable residents.

  4. September 19, 2026
    Commendation

    Commending the Nigerian Army’s Civil-Military Project at Eruwa General Hospital

    Hon. Ogunlade publicly applauded the Chief of Army Staff and Major General Oluremi A. Fadairo following the commissioning of a newly remodeled VIP/Physiotherapy section and a solar-powered water borehole facility at Eruwa General Hospital. He reiterated his core manifesto philosophy: “Leadership must be about service, responsibility, and results.”

  5. July 15, 2026
    Party Milestone

    Presentation of INEC Nomination Form to Party Apex Leaders

    Hon. Ogunlade officially presented his Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) nomination form to the apex leaders and elders of the All Progressives Congress (APC) in Ibarapa East. He received formal blessings, guidance, and strong backing from party leaders ahead of the general elections.

  6. July 12, 2026
    Strategic Alliance

    Strategic Political Alliance: MC Swagger Mobilises Support

    Prominent Oyo youth mobiliser and TAKE IT MOVEMENT Convener, Amb. (Dr.) Opeyemi Ayanrinde (MC Swagger), formally handed over his political structure to the APC and declared massive support for Hon. Babatunde Ogunlade under the banner “It’s Babs O’Clock in Ibarapa East,” solidifying youth backing for the 2027 bid.

  7. June 28, 2026
    Royal Congratulations

    Congratulating the Eleruwa of Eruwa on First Coronation Anniversary

    Hon. Ogunlade issued a warm congratulatory message to the Eleruwa of Eruwa, HRM Oba Samuel Adebayo Adegbola Ajobo Olurin I, commemorating his first coronation anniversary. He praised the monarch’s visionary leadership, dedication to peace, and continuous drive for cultural preservation and community development in Eruwaland.

  8. June 12, 2026
    Democracy Day

    Democracy Day Message: Commitment to Inclusive Democracy

    On the occasion of Democracy Day, Hon. Babatunde Ogunlade reaffirmed his unwavering commitment to inclusive democracy, grassroots-driven representation, and people-focused leadership for the people of Ibarapa East State Constituency. He emphasized that true governance must prioritize community welfare and active civic participation.
