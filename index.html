About BABS

A Leader Grounded in Service

Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade believes leadership is measured by the lives it improves. His approach is simple: listen first, act with integrity, and put the community at the centre of every decision.

Integrity

Honest, transparent and accountable leadership. Public trust is earned through consistent actions, open communication and respect for the rule of law.

Grassroots Empowerment

Real development starts in the community. BABS champions skills, enterprise and opportunities that put power and prosperity directly in the hands of the people.

Inclusive Representation

Women, youth, elders and persons with disabilities deserve a seat at the table. Every voice matters, and every community should see itself in leadership.

The Manifesto

Four Pillars for a Stronger Future

A clear, people-centred plan focused on the priorities that matter most to families, workers, students and businesses.

01

Economic Growth

  • Support small businesses, traders and artisans with access to finance and markets
  • Create jobs through agriculture, trade and local enterprise
  • Attract responsible investment and partnerships
02

Education & Youth

  • Expand scholarships, skills training and digital literacy
  • Improve learning environments for pupils and teachers
  • Open pathways to mentorship, leadership and entrepreneurship
03

Infrastructure

  • Prioritise roads, drainage, water and reliable electricity
  • Connect rural communities to markets and services
  • Deliver projects with transparency and community oversight
04

Healthcare Access

  • Strengthen primary healthcare centres in every community
  • Promote maternal, child and preventive health programmes
  • Make quality care more affordable and accessible
