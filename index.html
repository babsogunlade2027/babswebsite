The purpose of representation

Leadership should be visible in the work—and measurable in the answers.

Ibarapa East deserves representation that listens to communities, understands the issues affecting everyday life and follows public matters through the institutions responsible for addressing them. This platform sets out a proposed direction built on service, responsible advocacy and transparent reporting.

Meet BABS

A candidate shaped by community and enterprise.

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade is from Eruwa in Ibarapa East. His professional and business experience, community relationships and political involvement inform his stated interest in public service and legislative representation.

His proposed approach is anchored on listening to residents, identifying practical needs, engaging the relevant institutions and keeping the public informed about progress and outstanding challenges.

The FOCUS framework

Five priorities. One constituency-wide purpose.

The proposed agenda focuses on the foundations of a stronger local economy and more responsive public services. Each priority is intended to guide legislative advocacy, constituency engagement and scrutiny of public decisions.

FPriority 01

Farming & Food Security

Advocate for better access to agricultural information, inputs, cooperatives, storage, processing and farm-to-market connections.

OPriority 02

Opportunity & Jobs

Promote demand-led skills, apprenticeships, enterprise support, digital literacy and transparent access to relevant programmes.

CPriority 03

Community Infrastructure

Keep roads, safe water, drainage, electricity and maintenance needs on the agenda of the agencies and budgets responsible for delivery.

UPriority 04

Uplifting Education & Health

Advocate for evidence-based attention to schools, primary healthcare, learning conditions, essential services and referral gaps.

SPriority 05

Security & Social Cohesion

Support lawful collaboration, community dialogue, prevention and constructive engagement with constituted security agencies.
