Taking the Message to the Grassroots in Ward 1, Ibarapa East

24 Nov 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

The Hon. Babatunde Ogunlade (Babs) campaign team visited Ijesha Village, Baba Mogba Village, Agele Village, and local Fulani settlement camps in Ward 1, Ibarapa East to engage residents and community leaders ahead of 2027.

Our campaign team recently visited communities across Ward 1, Ibarapa East, sharing the vision and message of Hon. Babatunde Ogunlade (Babs) alongside all other All Progressives Congress (APC) candidates.

During the tour, the team engaged directly with residents, elders, and community leaders across key agrarian and pastoral settlements, including:

  • Ijesha Village
  • Baba Mogba Village
  • Agele Village
  • Local Fulani Settlement Camps

Inclusive representation starts with listening to every voice—from our farming communities to our pastoral settlements. We extend our sincere gratitude to the good people of Ward 1 for the warm reception, hospitality, and meaningful conversations as we work together toward a stronger, more united Ibarapa East.

Published by: Hon. Babatunde Ogunlade Campaign Media Team.
