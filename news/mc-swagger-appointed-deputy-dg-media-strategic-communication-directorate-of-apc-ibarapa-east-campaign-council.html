MC Swagger Appointed Deputy Director-General of APC Ibarapa East Campaign Communications

21 Sep 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

Amb. (Dr) Opeyemi Ayanrinde, known as MC Swagger, was reported appointed Deputy Director-General of Media and Strategic Communication for the APC Ibarapa East Campaign Council.

Amb. (Dr) Opeyemi Ayanrinde, popularly known as MC Swagger, was appointed Deputy Director-General of the Media and Strategic Communication Directorate of the APC Ibarapa East Campaign Council for the 2027 general elections, according to a report published on 21 September 2026.

The report described Ayanrinde as a broadcaster, entertainer and political mobiliser with more than two decades of experience across broadcasting, media and grassroots engagement. It also outlined previous roles in the Association of Nigerian Broadcasters, political mobilisation and campaign communications.

In an acceptance message shared on his social media platforms, Ayanrinde thanked APC leaders, stakeholders, executives and members across the ten wards of Ibarapa East for the confidence placed in him. He said he intended to apply his professional and media experience to the assignment.

The appointment and career details are presented as reported by SpyGist Media at the time of publication.

Source: SpyGist Media report, 21 September 2026.
