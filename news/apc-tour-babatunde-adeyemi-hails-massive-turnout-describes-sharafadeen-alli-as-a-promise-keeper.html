Babatunde Adeyemi Praises Turnout at APC Tour of Ibarapa

25 Oct 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

Babatunde Sunday Adeyemi praised APC supporters’ turnout during a party tour and expressed confidence in Senator Sharafadeen Alli’s governorship bid.

Babatunde Sunday Adeyemi has praised APC members and supporters who attended the party’s recent tour of Ibarapa, describing the turnout as an encouraging sign of grassroots engagement ahead of the 2027 elections.

Adeyemi said the tour gave Senator Sharafadeen Abiodun Alli an opportunity to interact with traditional rulers, religious leaders, young people, support groups and other stakeholders across the area.

He expressed confidence in Alli’s capacity to promote development and improve residents’ welfare if elected governor of Oyo State. Adeyemi described the senator as a promise keeper and urged party members and supporters to sustain their mobilisation.

The remarks represent Adeyemi’s assessment of the event and his political view of the governorship candidate.

Source: SpyGist Media report, 25 October 2026.
