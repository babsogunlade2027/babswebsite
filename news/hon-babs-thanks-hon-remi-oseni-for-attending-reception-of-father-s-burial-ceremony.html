Hon. BABS Thanks Hon. Remi Oseni for Attending Reception of Father’s Burial Ceremony

c. 17 Nov 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

Ogunlade issued a message of appreciation following Hon. Remi Oseni’s attendance at the reception held for his late father, Pa Idowu Ishola Ogunlade.

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade has expressed appreciation to Hon. Remi Oseni for attending the reception held as part of the burial ceremonies for his father, Pa Idowu Ishola Ogunlade.

The reception took place at Eleruwa Stadium, Eruwa. In the message, Ogunlade described Oseni’s presence as a gesture of solidarity with the family and thanked him for joining relatives, friends and well-wishers in honouring the late patriarch.

The statement was issued in Ogunlade’s name and identified him as the APC candidate for the Ibarapa East State Constituency seat in the Oyo State House of Assembly.

Source: Statement published on the official Facebook page of Hon. Ogunlade Babatunde Sunday (BABS).
