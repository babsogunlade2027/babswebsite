Ibarapa East APC Campaign Council Calls for Calm over Disputed Billboard Posters

27 Oct 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

The campaign council alleged that APM posters had been placed on an APC billboard and urged political actors to avoid actions that could heighten tensions.

The Ibarapa East All Progressives Congress (APC) Campaign Council has called for calm following what it described as the alleged placement of Allied Peoples Movement (APM) posters on an APC campaign billboard in the local government area.

In a statement issued by the council’s communications officials, the council characterised the alleged act as provocative and warned that disputes over campaign materials could create misunderstanding among political supporters. The allegation was made by the APC campaign council; the statement did not include an independent account from the APM or identify those responsible.

The council urged political parties and their supporters to respect one another, avoid inflammatory conduct and focus on issue-based campaigning. It also appealed to APC supporters to remain calm while the matter was addressed.

The council reaffirmed its stated commitment to peaceful political engagement and called for conduct that supports coexistence across Ibarapa East.

Source: Statement of the Ibarapa East APC Campaign Council, reported by SpyGist Media on 27 October 2026.
