Ogunlade Thanks Lukman Atunwa for Donation of Campaign-Branded Caps

22 Nov 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade has commended Hon. Lukman Atunwa for donating campaign-branded caps to the BABS Campaign Organization at the Ibarapa East APC Party House in Eruwa.

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade, popularly known as BABS and the All Progressives Congress (APC) candidate for the Oyo State House of Assembly in Ibarapa East State Constituency, has expressed his profound appreciation to Hon. Lukman Atunwa for his generous donation of campaign-branded caps in support of the BABS Campaign Organization.

The presentation, which took place on 22 November 2026 at the Ibarapa East APC Party House in Eruwa, brought together party leaders, stakeholders and supporters from across the constituency.

Hon. Ogunlade described the gesture as a timely and commendable demonstration of solidarity, loyalty and collective commitment to the success of the All Progressives Congress in Ibarapa East.

“On behalf of the entire BABS Campaign Organization and the progressive family across Eruwa, Lanlate and all wards in Ibarapa East State Constituency, I wish to express my heartfelt appreciation to Hon. Lukman Atunwa for this thoughtful and impactful show of support.

“This generous donation of campaign-branded caps will significantly strengthen our grassroots mobilization efforts, enhance our campaign visibility across communities and further energize our supporters as we build collective momentum towards securing purposeful, responsive and people-centred representation at the Oyo State House of Assembly.

“We deeply appreciate this demonstration of commitment and solidarity. It is through the collective efforts, dedication and unwavering support of committed party members and stakeholders that we can achieve our shared vision for a better Ibarapa East.”

Hon. Ogunlade further noted that gestures of generosity and support from dedicated party stalwarts such as Hon. Atunwa reinforce the unity, cooperation and collective determination necessary to advance good governance, inclusive representation and sustainable development across Ibarapa East.

He also called on party faithful, youth groups, women leaders, community mobilizers and other stakeholders across Eruwa and Lanlate to sustain the spirit of unity, remain focused and intensify their grassroots engagement in support of the party’s collective aspirations.

He reiterated that the strength of the BABS movement lies in unity, inclusiveness and the active participation of every committed supporter working towards a common goal.

The BABS Campaign Organization sincerely appreciates Hon. Lukman Atunwa for his generosity and continued commitment to the progressive family, while urging all stakeholders to sustain the momentum as the journey towards victory continues.

Source: Statement issued by the Media and Publicity Directorate, BABS Campaign Organization, dated 22 November 2026.
