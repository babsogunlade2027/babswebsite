Ibarapa East APC Youth Summit Holds with Adesoji Adedeji in Attendance

21 Oct 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

A campaign statement thanked participants and organisers following an Ibarapa East APC Youth Summit attended by deputy governorship candidate Hon. Adesoji Adedeji.

The Ibarapa East APC Youth Summit was held with the party’s deputy governorship candidate, Hon. Adesoji Adedeji, in attendance, according to a statement published by Hon. Babatunde Ogunlade’s campaign on 21 October 2026.

The statement thanked young participants, organisers, stakeholders and guests for attending. It described the summit as an opportunity to bring young party members together and encourage continued engagement.

The update did not provide the venue, attendance figures, programme agenda or resolutions adopted at the summit.

Source: Statement published on the official Facebook page of Hon. Ogunlade Babatunde Sunday (BABS).
