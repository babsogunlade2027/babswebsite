Ogunlade Congratulates Eleruwa on First Coronation Anniversary

28 Jun 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

Ogunlade congratulated the Eleruwa of Eruwa, Oba Samuel Adebayo Adegbola Ajobo Olurin I, on the first anniversary of his coronation.

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade has congratulated the Eleruwa of Eruwa, His Royal Majesty Oba Samuel Adebayo Adegbola Ajobo Olurin I, on the first anniversary of the monarch’s coronation.

In a congratulatory message, Ogunlade praised the traditional ruler’s leadership and commitment to cultural preservation, peace and community development. He described the traditional institution as an important partner in grassroots development.

The statement conveyed prayers for the monarch’s continued good health, wisdom and a long reign. It was issued in Ogunlade’s name as the APC candidate for the Ibarapa East State Constituency seat.

Source: Congratulatory statement reported by SpyGist Media on 28 June 2026.
