Ogunlade Holds Engagements with Water Base Group and Lanlate Community Association

24 Oct 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

The campaign reported meetings with the Water Base Group, Anunimorigba Landlord Association in Lanlate and other community groups.

Hon. Babatunde Ogunlade has continued a series of grassroots engagements, according to an update published by his campaign on 24 October 2026.

The update identified the Water Base Group and the Anunimorigba Landlord Association in Lanlate among the groups met. It also referred to engagements with other groups but did not provide their names, meeting dates or a detailed account of the discussions.

The campaign described the meetings as part of its effort to take its message to community organisations and residents. No formal resolutions or commitments arising from the meetings were listed in the update.

Source: Update published on the official Facebook page of Hon. Ogunlade Babatunde Sunday (BABS).
