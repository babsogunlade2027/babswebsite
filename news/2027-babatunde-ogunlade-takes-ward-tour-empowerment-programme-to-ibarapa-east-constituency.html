BABS Campaign Announces Ten-Ward Tour and Empowerment Programme

18 Sep 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

The campaign announced a proposed week-long tour across Ibarapa East’s ten wards from 20 to 27 September 2026, with community meetings and planned welfare support.

The BABS Media Team announced a ward tour and empowerment programme planned to cover all ten wards of Ibarapa East from 20 to 27 September 2026.

According to the published schedule, activities were to begin in Eruwa on 20 September, with engagements at LA School, Oke Oba, and LA School, Isaba. The itinerary also listed meetings in New Eruwa, Lanlate, Owode Layolayo, Anwarul-Islam School, Olonade’s Compound, Koonu’s Compound and Sango.

The schedule described the programme as a combination of campaign council activities, interactive sessions and the distribution of empowerment packages. It said the intended beneficiaries included widows, older residents and people facing hardship.

The announcement set out a planned programme. It should not be read as confirmation that every scheduled activity or distribution was subsequently completed.

Source: Schedule released by the BABS Media Team and reported by SpyGist Media on 18 September 2026.
