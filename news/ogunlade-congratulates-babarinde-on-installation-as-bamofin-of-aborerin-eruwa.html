Ogunlade Congratulates Babarinde on Installation as Bamofin of Aborerin Eruwa

21 Nov 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade congratulated Chief Barrister Samson Oyetunji Babarinde on his installation as Bamofin of Aborerin Eruwa, describing the honour as recognition of community service.

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade, the All Progressives Congress (APC) candidate for the Ibarapa East Constituency seat in the Oyo State House of Assembly, has congratulated Chief Barrister Samson Oyetunji Babarinde on his installation as the Bamofin of Aborerin Eruwa.

Babarinde was installed by the Baale of Aborerin Eruwa, High Chief Olaide Ojedapo. He is also a chieftain of the APC in the Ibarapa East Constituency.

In a congratulatory message, Ogunlade described the honour as a fitting recognition of a man who has served his community with dedication, integrity and a deep sense of responsibility.

“As a respected legal practitioner and a committed chieftain of our great party, the All Progressives Congress, in Ibarapa East Constituency, Chief Babarinde has consistently shown that leadership means service to the people,” Ogunlade said.

The candidate commended the Baale of Aborerin Eruwa, High Chief Olaide Ojedapo, for what he called a wise choice, and congratulated the people of Aborerin on the milestone.

Ogunlade expressed confidence that the new Bamofin would use his position to promote peace, unity and development in Aborerin, Eruwa and the whole of Ibarapa East.

“Our traditional institutions remain the custodians of our culture and values, and it is the duty of all of us to support and strengthen them,” he said.

He also prayed that God would grant the new Bamofin good health, wisdom and strength as he takes up the responsibility, and that his tenure would bring lasting progress to his people.

Ogunlade is the APC candidate for the Ibarapa East Constituency ahead of the 2027 general elections.

Source: Congratulatory statement issued by Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade, dated 21 November 2026.
