Hon. BABS Attends Unveiling of 2027 CAN Calendar in Aborerin Zone, Eruwa

c. 20 Nov 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

Hon. Babatunde Ogunlade attended the unveiling of the Christian Association of Nigeria, Aborerin Zone, 2027 calendar at Jesus Healing Christ Church Aladura, Oke Aseye, Eruwa.

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade, popularly known as BABS, attended the unveiling and launch of the 2027 calendar of the Christian Association of Nigeria (CAN), Aborerin Zone, Eruwa.

The event took place at Jesus Healing Christ Church Aladura, Oke Aseye, in Aborerin, Eruwa. In a statement published after the programme, Ogunlade commended the leadership of CAN Aborerin Zone for what he described as its continuing prayers and spiritual guidance for the community.

The statement presented the engagement as part of efforts to maintain relations with faith-based institutions in the area.

Source: Statement published on the official Facebook page of Hon. Ogunlade Babatunde Sunday (BABS).
