Ogunlade Commends Nigerian Army’s Civil-Military Project at Eruwa General Hospital

19 Sep 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

Ogunlade commended the Nigerian Army’s civil-military project at Eruwa General Hospital, which the release said included hospital remodelling and a solar-powered borehole.

Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade has commended the Nigerian Army following the commissioning of a Chief of Army Staff Civil-Military Cooperation project at Eruwa General Hospital.

The project was described in the statement as including the remodelling and reconstruction of the hospital’s VIP and physiotherapy section, as well as the provision of a solar-powered borehole intended to support water supply and healthcare delivery.

Ogunlade praised the Chief of Army Staff and the leadership of the Nigerian Army for the initiative. He also acknowledged Major General Oluremi A. Fadairo, identified in the statement as the General Officer Commanding 82 Division and Commander of Joint Task Force (South East), Operation UDO KA.

The candidate described the project as an example of institutional collaboration and said public infrastructure initiatives should respond to practical community needs. The project details and commendation are drawn from the statement issued in his name.

Source: Press statement reported by SpyGist Media on 19 September 2026.
