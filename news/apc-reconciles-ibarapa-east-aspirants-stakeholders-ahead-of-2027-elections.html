APC Holds Reconciliation Meeting for Ibarapa East Aspirants and Stakeholders

18 Jul 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

APC leaders, House of Assembly aspirants and stakeholders met in Ibarapa East as part of efforts to address internal differences ahead of the 2027 elections.

The All Progressives Congress (APC) leadership in Ibarapa East Local Government held a reconciliation meeting involving House of Assembly aspirants, party executives and stakeholders ahead of the 2027 general elections.

Pa Timothy Alarape Jolaoso, identified in the report as the leader of the APC in the Ibarapa zone, said reconciliation committees had been established across political levels. He disclosed that ten-member committees were constituted in each of Oyo State’s 32 State Assembly constituencies.

Participants discussed party unity and ways to strengthen internal engagement. Aspirants who spoke included Alhaji Lukman Atunwa, Barrister Silas Adepoju, Adegoke Waheed Olawale, Comrade Wale Ojewola, Mr Samin Adebare Adedeji, Mr Sodiq Egbedokun and Hon. Oladeni Bayo.

Several participants urged the party to maintain communication, engage aggrieved members and work towards reconciliation. Ogunlade, identified in the report as the party’s Ibarapa East Assembly candidate, pledged to work with party members regardless of previous differences.

The report records statements made at the meeting and the participants’ stated positions.

Source: SpyGist Media report by Sunday Adeniyi Adedokun, 18 July 2026.
