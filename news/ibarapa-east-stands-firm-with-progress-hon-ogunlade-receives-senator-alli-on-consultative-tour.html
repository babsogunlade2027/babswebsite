Ogunlade Receives Senator Alli during Ibarapa East Consultative Tour

21 Oct 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

Ogunlade reported receiving Senator Sharafadeen Alli and a delegation for consultations with traditional, religious, occupational and community stakeholders.

Hon. Babatunde Ogunlade said he received Oyo State APC governorship candidate Senator Sharafadeen Abiodun Alli and deputy governorship candidate Hon. Adesoji Adedeji during a consultative tour of Ibarapa East.

In his published statement, Ogunlade said the delegation also included senatorial candidate Hon. Aderemi Abass Oseni and federal constituency candidate Hon. Niyi Yusuf. The reported engagements involved traditional councils in Eruwa and Lanlate, Christian and Muslim communities, artisans, traders and pensioners.

The statement thanked traditional rulers, religious leaders, community representatives and residents who attended. Its description of the reception and level of support reflects the candidate’s account of the visit.

Source: Statement published on the official Facebook page of Hon. Ogunlade Babatunde Sunday (BABS).
