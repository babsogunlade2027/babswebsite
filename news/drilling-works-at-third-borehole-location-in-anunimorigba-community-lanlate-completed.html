Drilling Works at Third Borehole Location in Anunimorigba Community, Lanlate, Completed

10 Nov 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

A campaign update announced that drilling works at a third borehole location in Anunimorigba Community, Lanlate, had been completed.

A campaign update issued on 10 November 2026 announced the completion of drilling works at a third borehole location in Anunimorigba Community, Lanlate.

The announcement thanked people involved in the effort and described the work as part of an initiative intended to improve access to clean water in the area.

The update did not provide technical details, commissioning arrangements or information on when the facility would become operational. The completion statement is therefore presented as reported by the campaign.

Source: Campaign update published on the official Facebook page of Hon. Ogunlade Babatunde Sunday (BABS).
