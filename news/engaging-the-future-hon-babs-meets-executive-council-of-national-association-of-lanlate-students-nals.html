Ogunlade Meets Executive Council of National Association of Lanlate Students

22 Oct 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

Ogunlade, accompanied by members of the Patriotic Progressives Network, met the executive council of the National Association of Lanlate Students.

Hon. Babatunde Ogunlade met with the executive council of the National Association of Lanlate Students (NALS) during an engagement in Ibarapa East, according to a campaign update published on 22 October 2026.

The statement said Ogunlade was accompanied by Hon. Lanre Olaosegba, Hon. Oloyede Raji and members of the Patriotic Progressives Network (PPN), Ibarapa East.

The campaign described the meeting as an opportunity to engage student representatives and hear youth perspectives. The published update did not provide a list of issues raised by the students or specific agreements reached at the meeting.

Source: Update published on the official Facebook page of Hon. Ogunlade Babatunde Sunday (BABS).
