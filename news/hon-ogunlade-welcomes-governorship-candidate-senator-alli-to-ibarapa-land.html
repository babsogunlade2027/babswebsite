Ogunlade Welcomes Senator Sharafadeen Alli to Ibarapa

19 Oct 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

Ogunlade issued a welcome message ahead of Senator Sharafadeen Alli’s visit to Ibarapa.

Hon. Babatunde Ogunlade has welcomed Senator Sharafadeen Abiodun Alli to Ibarapa in a message published ahead of the governorship candidate’s visit to the area.

In the statement, Ogunlade said the visit offered an opportunity to engage communities and expressed support for Alli’s stated ambition to lead Oyo State. He also called on residents and party members to receive the delegation.

The message was issued in Ogunlade’s capacity as the APC candidate for the Ibarapa East State Constituency seat in the Oyo State House of Assembly.

Source: Statement published on the official Facebook page of Hon. Ogunlade Babatunde Sunday (BABS).
