Ibarapa East APC Leaders Meet Ogunlade after INEC Nomination Form Presentation

15 Jul 2026·Ibarapa East, Oyo State

Ogunlade presented his INEC nomination form to senior APC leaders in Ibarapa East and sought their advice and support ahead of the 2027 election.

Senior All Progressives Congress (APC) figures in Ibarapa East met with the party’s House of Assembly candidate, Hon. Babatunde Sunday Ogunlade, after he presented his Independent National Electoral Commission (INEC) nomination form to the party’s local leadership.

The presentation took place during a consultative visit to Pa Timothy Alarape Jolaoso and Pa Dayo Adeola. The report said Ogunlade sought their prayers, guidance and support as preparations continued for the 2027 Oyo State House of Assembly election.

Ogunlade said the visit was intended to demonstrate respect for party elders and keep them involved in the next phase of his campaign. The leaders encouraged party members to remain united and focused.

The report identified the APC local government chairman, Comrade Dele Belolari, Ward 2 chairman Mr Peter Aderoju and other party executives and stakeholders as attendees.

Source: SpyGist Media report, 15 July 2026.
