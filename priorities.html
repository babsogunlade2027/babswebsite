The Blueprint

Proposed priorities for Ibarapa East

These priorities are proposals and intentions for policy attention and advocacy. They are subject to consultation, legislative processes, partnerships and available resources, and are not claims of completed work.
01

Farming & food security

Support the people who feed Ibarapa East.

  • Advocate for farmer support and access to inputs
  • Promote better market access for local produce
  • Encourage practical agricultural partnerships
02

Opportunity & jobs

Open doors for young people and small businesses.

  • Promote vocational and skills pathways
  • Encourage small-business and enterprise support
  • Advocate for job-creating partnerships
03

Community infrastructure

Basic facilities that communities rely on every day.

  • Promote attention to local roads and mobility
  • Advocate for maintained public facilities
  • Engage communities on local project needs
04

Uplifting education & health

Stronger learning environments and primary healthcare.

  • Support learning environments for young people
  • Advocate for stronger primary healthcare
  • Promote health awareness and responsive services
05

Security & social cohesion

A safer, more united Ibarapa East.

  • Encourage community-level dialogue and cooperation
  • Support peaceful, respectful engagement across wards
  • Advocate for responsive public safety coordination
